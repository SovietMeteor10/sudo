welcome to sudo

private by default

sudo is a messenger built so the server can't read what you say. only your devices can.

your account lives on your devices, not on the server. if you sign out without a backup, your account on this browser is gone.

this browser is one device

when you create an account, the keys live on this browser. it counts as one of your devices.

you can pair another browser or phone later. all your devices share the same account, but each keeps its own copy of the keys.

linked devices + revoke

to add another device, open settings → linked devices and scan the QR code. the new device gets its own keys, derived from yours.

if a device is lost or stolen, you can revoke it. revoke means: that device can't send or read messages anymore. the rest of your devices stay signed in.

disappearing messages + push

inside any chat, the clock icon turns on disappearing messages. messages older than the timer are erased from every device in that conversation.

push notifications are off by default. you can turn them on in settings. notifications only show that you got a message — never the content.

you're ready

if you ever want to back up your account, settings → backup account saves an encrypted file you can restore on a new device.

reset this browser clears everything from this device only. it doesn't touch your other devices or anyone you've chatted with.

danger zone
